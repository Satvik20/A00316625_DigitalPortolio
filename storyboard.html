PART 03 / INDUSTRY EXPERIENCE

Ericsson and HPE

“At Ericsson, I worked as a Software Engineer Intern with LangChain, Langflow, Langfuse and LangGraph, helping integrate AI workflows. I resolved bugs, rewrote and optimised code, worked in a hybrid team with daily stand-ups, and mentored sixth-year students in software engineering principles. During my January–August 2026 HPE Software AI Engineer internship, I developed a Jira assistant using Python webhooks to respond to comments and triage bugs, and Jenkins analysis that summarises thousands of logs and emails key issues. I designed self-healing tests that try a dynamic fix after a locator fails, rerun the test and open a pull request after successful recovery. I also automated cluster end-to-end runs, investigated flaky tests and flagged critical defects. My key lesson: verify automation before relying on it.”
